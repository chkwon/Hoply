Hoply Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-16

Hoply (“the app”) does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. This policy describes how the app handles data and what your rights are.

1. Information we collect

The app collects no personal information, identifiers, or usage logs. There is no account, sign-up, or sign-in.

2. How documents are processed

Every .hwp · .hwpx document you open in the app is parsed and rendered locally on your device. Document content is never sent to an external server, nor uploaded to any cloud storage or analytics service.

3. Network traffic

The app makes no network requests of its own. When you explicitly choose to share (e.g. emailing the original document or a PDF) or to print (AirPrint), the standard iOS system features handle the transfer; in that case the destination app or service's policy applies.

4. Third-party analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking technology. It does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.

5. Permissions

The app only accesses documents you explicitly hand to it through the Files app or “Open In…”. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

6. Children

The app behaves identically for all users, including children under 13, and collects no information from anyone — including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text and the effective date above will be published on this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store description or in the release notes (CHANGELOG.md).

8. Contact
